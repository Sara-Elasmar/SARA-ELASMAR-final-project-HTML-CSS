DOCUMENTATION GUIDE

Recognizing & Preserving Evidence

Violence takes many forms. Knowing what to record helps you build a factual timeline that can support your safety.

01

PHYSICAL VIOLENCE

Examples:

  • Seek medical attention.
  • Request and preserve medical reports.
  • Photograph injuries or damage when safe.
  • Keep relevant documents.

What to do:

  • Seek medical attention.
  • Request and preserve medical reports.
  • Photograph injuries or damage when safe.
  • Keep relevant documents.

02

DIGITAL VIOLENCE

Examples:

  • Threatening messages.
  • Online harassment.
  • Blackmail.
  • Non-consensual sharing of intimate images.

What to do:

  • Capture screenshots.
  • Preserve original messages.
  • Record dates, usernames, and phone numbers.
  • Avoid deleting original conversations or files.

03

PSYCHOLOGICAL VIOLENCE OR FORCED EVICTION

Examples:

  • Repeated intimidation.
  • Threats.
  • Coercive control.
  • Being forcibly removed from your home.

What to do:

  • Document incidents and dates.
  • Preserve emails, communications.
  • Record relevant witness information.
  • Seek professional and legal assistance.
